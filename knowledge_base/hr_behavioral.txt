HR & BEHAVIORAL INTERVIEW KNOWLEDGE BASE
=========================================

## Common HR Interview Questions & Best Answers

### Tell Me About Yourself
Framework:
1. Present: Current role and key responsibilities
2. Past: Most relevant experience and achievements
3. Future: Why this role excites you
Keep it to 2-3 minutes. Be professional but personable.

### Why Do You Want This Job?
- Research the company's mission, products, culture
- Connect your skills to the role requirements
- Show genuine enthusiasm, not just need for a job
- Mention specific aspects of the company that appeal to you

### Greatest Strength
- Choose a strength relevant to the job
- Provide a specific example with measurable impact
- Tie it to how it benefits the employer

### Greatest Weakness
- Choose a real weakness (not a disguised strength)
- Show self-awareness and growth mindset
- Describe concrete steps you've taken to improve
- Example: "I used to struggle with delegation, so I started using project management tools and weekly check-ins..."

## STAR Method Framework

### Situation
- Set the scene with enough context
- Brief but specific — when, where, what was the challenge

### Task
- Your role and responsibility in the situation
- What was expected of you?

### Action
- Focus on YOUR actions (use "I" not "we")
- Be specific about what you did
- Walk through your decision-making process

### Result
- Quantify outcomes where possible (%, $, time saved)
- Mention what you learned
- Keep it positive, even for failure stories

## Behavioral Question Banks

### Leadership
- Tell me about a time you led a team through a difficult situation
- Describe a time you had to make an unpopular decision
- How do you motivate team members who are underperforming?

### Conflict Resolution
- Describe a disagreement with a colleague and how you resolved it
- Tell me about a time a project failed — what did you do?
- How do you handle conflicting priorities from multiple stakeholders?

### Problem Solving
- Tell me about the most complex problem you've solved
- Describe a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information
- Walk me through a creative solution you developed

### Adaptability
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something new quickly
- How have you handled major changes in your work environment?
- Describe a time you failed — what did you learn?

### Teamwork
- Tell me about a successful team project you contributed to
- How do you handle a team member not pulling their weight?
- Describe your communication style and how it helps teams

## Salary Negotiation

### Research
- Know your market value (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, LinkedIn Salary)
- Consider total compensation: base, bonus, equity, benefits
- Know the company's pay bands if possible

### Negotiation Tactics
- Never give the first number if possible
- Give a range with your target at the bottom
- "Based on my research and experience, I'm looking for $X-$Y"
- Consider the entire offer, not just base salary

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer

### Role & Team
- What does success look like in this role after 90 days?
- What are the biggest challenges facing the team right now?
- How would you describe the team culture?

### Growth & Development
- What does the career path look like from this position?
- Are there opportunities for learning and professional development?

### Company & Strategy
- What are the company's top priorities over the next 12 months?
- How has the team evolved over the past year?

---

## Cultural Fit Questions

### Work Style
- Do you prefer working independently or collaboratively?
- How do you prioritize when everything feels urgent?
- Describe your ideal work environment

### Values Alignment
- What motivates you most in your work?
- How do you define professional success?
- Why are you leaving your current role?
